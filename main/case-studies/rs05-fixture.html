RS05 fixture measurements#

The RS05 study uses a pendulum arm with interchangeable tip weights. The modeled tip mass includes every fastener that moves with the weight. These measurements describe that assembly; use your own values for another bench.

The corresponding files are configs/fixtures/pendulum_<weight>g_200mm.toml. They store masses in kilograms, including weight_mass, bolt_mass, nut_mass and the total mass. The loader checks that the components sum to the total.

Fixture used for identification#

The fitting model places the complete tip assembly at a single point at radius L = 0.2 m. It uses

\[ J_f=mL^2+J_a+J_h,\qquad G_\mathrm{horizontal}=9.80665\,(mL+m_a c_a). \]

Fixture used for CAD replay#

The finite CAD fixture includes the individual components’ own inertias. The identified_point option preserves the inertia assumed during fitting while showing the CAD geometry. Comparing these two choices isolates the consequence of the point-load approximation.

The full research archive stores geometry and the mass-property manifest in data/rs05/assets/bench/. Those files are not included in the small offline example. Historical corrections to pilot metadata are retained in the archive; the formal v3 payloads and their hashes remain unchanged.
