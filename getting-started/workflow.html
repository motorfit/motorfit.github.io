Workflow#

A study connects a mounted motor, its controller, a measured fixture and a declared set of recordings. MotorFit supports five driver families, two control-input paths and a pendulum rig. Motor support describes the available protocols and their validation coverage.

Plan the experiment#

Record the motor identity, controller mode and units, supply setting, coordinate convention, fixture geometry and measured masses. Choose loads, gains, trajectories and durations that cover the intended use. A longer run alone does not establish accuracy; use independent conditions and repetitions.

Assign recordings to training, model-selection validation and final test before fitting. Samples from the same trajectory are not independent experiments. Keep those split assignments with the data.

Keep the study together#

Use an ignored local directory such as:

data/my-motor/
  raw/train/             # completed recordings and their original evidence
  raw/validation/
  raw/test/
  processed/             # the same split structure after resampling
  candidates/            # fitted models and selection records
  results/               # fixed-model evaluations, plots and reports

Save the configuration, input hashes and runtime versions with the study. Keep failed runs and their evidence too. Use outputs/ for disposable demonstrations or build products.

Review completion, faults and timing before processing. In RobStride recordings, load is firmware-reported nominal torque, not a shaft torque sensor. Preserve independent wire captures when the study requires a separate timing audit.

Freeze and report#

Freeze the selected parameter file, split list, input hashes, controller and fixture definitions, software versions and selection rule before final evaluation. A later parameter or selection change starts a new study.

Report agreement with measurements separately from agreement between simulators. Include the operating conditions, per-record errors and remaining unexplained behavior. The RS05 study is a worked example with a portable manifest and saved predictions. A manifest indexes prepared data; there is no command that automatically designs the split or creates a complete study.
