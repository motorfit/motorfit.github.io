MotorFit#

Record a motor. Identify its dynamics. Validate the model.

MotorFit extends Rhoban BAM with native motor drivers, bench-data acquisition and model validation. Collect commands and measured motion, identify actuator dynamics, and compare model predictions with recordings and supported physics backends.

Start with a complete example#

Install MotorFit and run the offline example. Three recorded experiments and a frozen model let you check the workflow without a motor or GPU.

When you are ready to collect data, follow the experiment workflow and configure your own motor.
